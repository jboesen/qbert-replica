Every strategy is compared to a normal team in the identical league, draft slot and schedule, averaged over 1,200 matched team pairs for each kind of rival (20 drafts, 12 teams, 5 seasons, 20 schedules each). Weekly win rate is the record you’d have if you played every other team every week, so a lucky or unlucky schedule doesn’t count. Numbers are percentage points of change. Realistic rivals stray from the rankings a bit, like real people; strict rivals follow them exactly. A second AI reviewer checked the test design before anything ran.
